What we collect

When you request a quote or book a service, we collect the information you type in: your name, phone number, email (optional), address or city, the services you need and any notes about your property.

How we use it

Only to reply to your request, give you an estimate, schedule and confirm your service, and contact you about that job. We do not sell or rent your information, and we do not send marketing messages unless you ask us to.

Text messages and calls

If you choose text, call or WhatsApp as your preferred contact, we'll use that number to reach you about your request. Reply STOP to any text message and we won't text you again.

Advertising measurement

This site uses the Meta Pixel to measure how well our Facebook and Instagram ads work (for example, whether someone who saw an ad later asked for a quote). Meta may use cookies for this. You can control ad preferences in your Facebook or Instagram settings.
